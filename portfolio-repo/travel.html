✦ AMALIEJUDD

A designer, swimmer & runner optimising for fun.

I’m a Senior UX Designer based in Sydney with around eight years of experience turning gnarly problems into clear, considered products.

My work spans retail, energy, utilities and aviation, mostly large-scale internal tools and platforms, always with a focus on the people actually using the thing. I started out studying graphic design at the University of Wollongong before finishing a Bachelor of Design Computing at the University of Sydney.

I joined Designit in January 2022 and have since led end-to-end design for WooliesX, Air New Zealand, Origin Energy and Sydney Water. I work across research, interaction design, design systems and prototyping, and I’m at my best embedded in a team who care about getting the details right.

To befriend me or hire me, reach out on LinkedIn, by email, or download my CV.
