More about me!

I’m a Senior UX Designer based in Sydney with around eight years of experience turning complex problems into clear, considered products. My work spans retail, energy, utilities and aviation, mostly large-scale internal tools and platforms, always with a focus on people.
I started out studying graphic design at the University of Wollongong before finishing a Bachelor of Design Computing at the University of Sydney.

Outside of work I’m usually in the water, planning my next holiday, or fixated on a puzzle. I grew up in Sydney and still live in the Inner West, and most weekends involve a long dog walk and playing PC games with my husband. I’m also learning to golf.

Open to senior UX and product design roles in Sydney and beyond.

To befriend me or hire me, reach out on LinkedIn, by email, or download my CV—can’t wait to meet you!
