✦ AMALIEJUDD

Designing complex systems that feel simple to use.

I’m a Senior UX Designer based in Sydney with around eight years of experience turning gnarly problems into clear, considered products. My work spans retail, energy, utilities and aviation — mostly large-scale internal tools and platforms, always with a focus on the people actually using the thing.

I started out studying graphic design at the University of Wollongong before finishing a Bachelor of Design Computing at the University of Sydney. Before that it was The Sims teaching me about space and layout, WordArt introducing me to type, and building Tumblr themes teaching me just enough HTML and CSS to still be useful in a conversation with engineers.

I joined Designit in January 2022 and have since led end-to-end design for WooliesX, Air New Zealand, Origin Energy and Sydney Water. I work across research, interaction design, design systems and prototyping, and I’m at my best embedded in a team who care about getting the details right.

Open to senior UX and product design roles in Sydney and beyond.

To befriend me or hire me, reach out on LinkedIn, by email, or download my CV—can’t wait to meet you!
